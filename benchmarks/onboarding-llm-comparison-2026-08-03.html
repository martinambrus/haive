What got better

  • Citation discipline is now near-universal at the top. Both August runs resolve 100% of their file:line claims (715 and 511 of them) — no invented paths, no line numbers past end-of-file. The fabricated-signature era of July-10 Codex (six invented Db methods) has no successor in this round.
  • The elocks subsystem finally has definitive coverage. Both new runs independently reconstruct the asymmetric-freshness bug: the own-row Delete uses timeout > $time_delete (thememanager_admin.php:844) while the foreign-lock SELECT at :845 has no freshness filter at all. DeepSeek states the polarity inversion outright; Kimi additionally traces the heartbeat re-arm and the per-theme INSERT mirrors.
  • The Codex line kept climbing across rounds: o3 → gpt-5.5 fixed the invented Db signatures and the edit-lock misread; gpt-5.6-sol catches the Db::Update inverted return at every effort level, raised its family floor to 8.20 and produced the benchmark's first winning top-effort run (max, 8.55).

What got worse / didn't move

  • Neither Ollama refresh beats its own predecessor. Kimi K2.7 scored 8.30 (June) and 8.28 (July-10); kimi-k3 lands at 8.05. June's deepseek-v4-pro scored 8.60; deepseek-v4-flash lands at 7.83 — though flash is the fast tier of that family, not a straight successor, so that pair is a tier comparison rather than a regression.
  • Global routing is still the ceiling, and it widened. 34 of 44 runs promoted nothing. Both August runs sit on the floor, which is why 09_6_4-global-kb-merge skipped in both — with zero promoted drafts there was no colliding pair to merge.
  • Link hygiene regressed to a new worst. DeepSeek V4 Flash breaks 44 of 272 relative links (16%), overtaking Sonnet-5-high's ~20 as the field record, and does it by repeating a wrong directory name (../form-wizard-system/ against the real form-wizard/) rather than by volume alone.

09Notable defects & patterns

The Db::Update landmine

Still the single most discriminating fact. Update() returns true on failure (database.php:456) and false on success (:459). Codex 5.6 catches it at all six efforts — the only family to do so. Both August runs miss it: Kimi's API_REFERENCE details dataset quoting and CheckFieldSize but never states polarity; DeepSeek reduces the method to “UPDATE with field size check”. Neither cites database.php:456/459 anywhere. Haiku low remains the only run to actively reverse it.
Jest trap — three July runs materialised a jest-specialist for a project with no tests. Neither August run falls for it: both write a TESTING_STANDARDS.md asserting no test infrastructure exists and ship no jest agent, but neither names Jest as the false signal, so both land short of Codex-5.6 xhigh, which identified it as the Polish/Slovenian word “jest” in bundled FCKeditor language files.
Counting errors — the 23-table schema keeps tripping models. Codex-5.6 xhigh counted “20 UTF-8 MyISAM tables”; DeepSeek V4 Flash writes “creates 18 tables” and then lists all 23 in the same sentence. Kimi K3 counts 23 correctly and adds the utf8/utf8_bin collation detail.

Hygiene & routing

Sandbox-path leaks: Sonnet-5 max ships 5 literal /haive/workdir paths; Fable xhigh 4; Kimi K2.7 (Jul-10) 9 in one skill. DeepSeek V4 Flash leaks exactly one — but bakes it into a runnable grep -rn ... /haive/workdir/ command, so it is a copy-paste trap rather than stray prose. Kimi K3 is leak-free.
Harness-value bleed keeps finding new surfaces. Codex-5.6 high branded 3 of its 6 global promotions “MariaDB 10”. Kimi K3 names an entire local KB facet family mariadb and then prescribes InnoDB transactions over LOCK TABLES for an all-MyISAM app — accurate modern-DB advice aimed at the wrong database. Its prose, by contrast, is the field's cleanest separation of sandbox from application.
Broken cross-skill links remain endemic and uncorrelated with spend (0 to 44 per run). The deterministic verify step checks structure, not link targets. Codex 5.6 posts perfect 0/317 and 0/163 sweeps at one end; DeepSeek V4 Flash sets a new worst at 44/272 while spending the least compute of any August run.
Effort past the peak buys defects, not quality for the claude families: every xhigh run lands below its family peak, and Sonnet-5/4.6/Haiku max all regress. The single counterexample is Codex-5.6 max — and even there, 2.8× the compute of high bought +0.20 composite.

10Recommendations

  1. Highest-stakes onboarding: Sonnet 5 at high effort. Still the top composite of all 44 runs (8.80) after two further rounds, and the only run combining a near-perfect KB with four clean, version-correct global promotions. Budget ~40 active CLI minutes; tidy the redundant skill cross-links.
  2. Best value: Sonnet 5 at medium (8.38 in ~26 min), with GLM-5.2 via Ollama (Jul-10) as the standing cheap alternative (8.25 in 31.8 min). Neither promotes globally; add an explicit promotion pass.
  3. When accuracy on API contracts matters most: Codex gpt-5.6-sol. The only family that catches the Db::Update inverted return at every effort, with zero fabricated APIs and mostly perfect link hygiene across ~250 files. Watch the high run's Test frameworks: jest line in AGENTS.md and the “MariaDB 10” branding in its promoted articles.
  4. The new Ollama refreshes are usable, but not an upgrade. Pick kimi-k3 when you want citation-exact subsystem documentation and clean sandbox hygiene (0 leaks, 23 tables right, best edit-lock write-up); pick deepseek-v4-flash when compute is the binding constraint (27.1 min, the cheapest of the two) and budget a link-repair pass afterwards. Neither should be chosen over its predecessor line on quality alone.
  5. The effort dial: stop at each family's peak — never xhigh. No family peaks at the xhigh tier, and only Codex 5.6 rewards max. Opus and Fable both peak at medium.
  6. Do not use Haiku 4.5 for legacy onboarding. A full tier below (best 6.75): it reverses the Update contract, fabricates escaping, invents schema names, hallucinates a Jest agent at max — and at xhigh ships no testing doc at all.

▸ Methodology & caveats
  • Ground truth and source identity. Every one of the 44 leaderboard runs onboarded a byte-identical copy of the same Redaction System source. This was re-verified for this edition, not assumed: for each repository the import commit's tree was listed with git ls-tree -r, Haive-generated paths (.claude/, .agents/, .codex/, .haive*, .ddev/, AGENTS.md, CLAUDE.md, .gitignore) were excluded, and the remaining 828 application blobs were hashed. All 44 produce the identical digest. Two further candidates, rs_test_gl and rs_test_gh (14 July, Haiku Low), fail that raw digest on 362 of 828 blobs but pass once line endings are normalised — they are LF-normalised copies of the same corpus (verified: tr -d '\r' then re-hash yields the identical whole-tree digest as the other 44). They are excluded on provenance, not source: both are duplicate Haiku-Low control runs created back-to-back to exercise the global-KB supersede path, not distinct model or effort points, and neither promoted anything.
  • Audit dossier. Findings were judged against the real files and one shared, source-verified fact dossier, each item re-confirmed for this edition: the Db::Update inverted return (return true on error at database.php:456, return false on success at :459), the elocks edit-lock table and its asymmetric freshness handling (thememanager_admin.php:844/845), SECHASH = md5(HTTP_USER_AGENT.REMOTE_ADDR) at installer/init.php:143 (IP/UA, not CSRF), no rank/CSRF API, encryptPW = md5(crypt($pw, substr($salt,0,2))) with default salt salted_sardine at functions.php:988, the is_numeric-guarded math-captcha eval() at functions.php:1301, the six /e preg_replace sites at functions.php:368-373, and the 23-table schema. Artifact count alone earned no credit.
  • Link and citation measurement (new this edition). For the two August runs both figures are measured, not estimated. Every relative markdown link under .claude/knowledge_base/ and .claude/skills/ was resolved against the filesystem; every file.php:NNN citation was checked for file existence (matching by basename anywhere in the repo, so a bare form_submit.php resolves to actions/form_submit.php) and for the line number falling within the file. Kimi K3: 5/109 links broken, 715 citations, 0 unresolvable. DeepSeek V4 Flash: 44/272 links broken, 511 citations, 0 unresolvable.
  • Model identity. Verified from each run's CLI init event rather than the task title or the cli_providers.model text column, which is known to go stale (the z.ai rows still read glm-4.6 while the runs were glm-5.2[1m]). The August runs were launched as claude --model kimi-k3:cloud and --model deepseek-v4-flash:cloud, echoed back in the system/init event of each stream log. Neither Ollama provider exposes a reasoning-effort setting, so both are single points, not sweeps.
  • Detection (20%). Audited LLM output plus AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md; deterministic confirmation-form values (mariadb 10.11, apache) were separated from model judgment, and copying them into LLM-authored KB was penalised.
  • KB (25%), Skills (25%), Agents (15%). Read every KB file (recursive), every SKILL.md, and only the accepted.source = "llm" discovered agents (the ~28 shared scan/template agents excluded). Both August runs discovered exactly three (fckeditor, mysql, legacy-php), read from the 06_5-agent-discovery step output rather than inferred from filenames.
  • Global KB (15%). Counted task-attributed promotions in haive_kb_global.global_kb_entries across every status. The query was calibrated against published values before use (Sonnet-5 high = 4, Codex-5.6 high = 6, Opus xhigh = 1, all exact). Zero promotions = the 6.0 neutral floor (no contamination, missed reuse), not a six-out-of-ten quality verdict. Both August runs returned zero rows. The consequent skip of 09_6_4-global-kb-merge in both is a downstream effect, not a cause: that step's shouldRun gate asks whether any promoted draft collides with an existing entry, so no promotions means no pairs to merge.
  • Cost. Active CLI minutes = sum of cli_invocations.duration_ms across all rows incl. superseded retries; output tokens and retry counts follow the same rule. The query was validated by reproducing published figures exactly for known runs before being applied to new ones (Sonnet-5 high 24 inv / 40.5 min / 184,147 tok; and all six July-10 runs to the decimal). Raw input/total tokens excluded (providers cache differently).
  • Wall time is contextual only and omitted from rankings. Kimi K3's 1,507 wall minutes are ~25 hours of unattended form-gate idle against 46.5 minutes of actual compute — the same pattern as the three gpt-5.5 Codex runs (~40h) and the Fable overnight runs. Speed and value use active CLI minutes only.
  • Pipeline drift between rounds. The step list is not frozen: the August runs execute 26 steps against the July-13/14 runs' 25, the added step being 13-onboarding-push (skipped for both, as these repositories have no remote). Both August runs also skipped 09_3-qa-review, 09_5b-skill-repair, 09_6_4 and 09_6_5. Cross-round deltas therefore mix model change with pipeline change and are reported per model line rather than pooled.
  • Calibration. Dimension scores are evidence-calibrated 0.5-point judgments, not measurements; composite decimals come only from the published weights. The two August runs were scored against the previously published runs as fixed anchors — in particular the promotion-quality ladder and the treatment of naming echoes (a harness-named facet whose content is accurate caps detection rather than KB, matching how Sonnet-5 high and Codex-5.6 low were handled).
  • What is excluded. Cancelled and failed onboarding tasks; the two rs_test_gl/rs_test_gh runs (content-identical source, excluded as duplicate Haiku-Low feature-test controls — see above); and the 23 June-round tasks whose repositories no longer exist on disk, which appear only as the unverifiable historical table in section 11.
